Academic Matrix Interpretation

How to explain this matrix to project evaluators and viva examiners.

True Negatives (TN):

Legitimate job & internship listings correctly predicted as safe. Candidates can proceed to apply with peace of mind.

True Positives (TP):

Scam & fraudulent listings correctly identified and intercepted before applicants lose money or private data.

False Positives (FP):

A legitimate company flagged as a scam (Type I error). Minimized by measuring high Precision.

False Negatives (FN):

A dangerous scam misclassified as legitimate (Type II error). Minimized by measuring high Recall, ensuring student safety.
