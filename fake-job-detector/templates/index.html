💡 How This Project Detects Scams:

Machine Learning (NLP)

2

Scam Keyword Rules

Automatically flags upfront registration fees, security deposits, cashier check schemes, and promises of high pay with no interview.

3

Contact Channel Check

Checks if the recruiter uses free personal emails (@gmail.com, @yahoo.com) or moves conversations to secret Telegram/WhatsApp chats.
